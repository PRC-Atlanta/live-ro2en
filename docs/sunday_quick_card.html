Sunday quick card

Live RO2EN · Romanian ⇄ English captions, offline

Before the service

  1. Open Live RO2EN 10 minutes early. Wait until the dial says START.
  2. Check the three lights above the dial: Sound, ProPresenter, Pace are green.
  3. Ask the sound desk to bring the mixer send up; the meter should sit in the green GOOD band while someone speaks.
  4. Guest speaker or unusual names? Press T and type them, one per line.
  5. Optional: Rehearsal plays a recording through to the big screen.

Start and stop

SpaceStart captions (or click the dial).
Space SpaceStop: press twice, or hold the dial.

Something wrong on screen? Press H. Captions hide at once; press H again to show them.

During the service

HHide or show captions
EscBlank the big screen until the next phrase
BackspaceRemove the last caption
EFix the last caption
TToday's names & words
1 2 3Speaker: Auto-detect / Romanian / English
QQuality: Quickest or Best
RReconnect to ProPresenter

After the service

  1. Stop with Space Space.
  2. Click After in the toolbar. Rate how it went (1–5) and tick what was off. This helps tune it.
  3. Want the text? Export saves it to the Desktop (txt, subtitles or Word).
  4. Leave the app open, or quit it. Nothing else to do.

If a coloured bar appears above the dial

It says what is wrong. Press its button first; it does the right thing.

No sound is coming inCheck the cable and that the mixer send is up. The meter moves again once sound arrives.
Can't reach ProPresenterCaptions still show in the app. In ProPresenter: Settings → Network → Enable Network. Then press Try again.
Captions running behindThey catch up by themselves. Press Switch to Quickest if it keeps happening.
The translator stoppedPress Start again.
Microphone not allowedSystem Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → turn on Live RO2EN.
Anything elseOpen Help in the app. Copy diagnostics there saves a file to send to whoever looks after the app.
Everything runs on this Mac; no internet is needed during the service. Settings (sound input, ProPresenter, usual language) are in the app under Settings.
