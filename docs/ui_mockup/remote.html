Live Remote: the toolbar for the ProPresenter Mac

A small floating toolbar that stays above ProPresenter without taking focus from it. It finds the captions Mac on the network by itself and drives it with the same controls as the main app. Drag it by the LT handle. Right-click it, or use ⋯, to switch between horizontal and vertical.

1 · Try it

ProPresenter

The buttons work here: Hide turns the toolbar amber, Hold blue. The shortcuts work in any app, ProPresenter included, without the toolbar needing focus: ⌃⌥H hide/show, ⌃⌥G hold, ⌃⌥K clear, ⌃⌥Z remove last, ⌃⌥S song, ⌃⌥N names & words. You can change them in the menu.

2 · Every state

From the top: captioning · hidden (nothing reaches ProPresenter) · holding the current caption · the main app is ready but not captioning · the captions Mac can't be reached (buttons greyed out, and it retries by itself). Vertical, on the right: no caption line, and the LED stands in for the status text.

Sent · 14 names for today

Lists are kept on the ProPresenter Mac, so whoever builds the service there can prepare them ahead of time. Send for today replaces today’s names on the captions Mac, the same as typing them into its Names & words box. They take effect from the next phrase, even mid-service. Get the list in use pulls back what the captions Mac has now, for editing or saving. The badge on the Names button shows how many are in use.
