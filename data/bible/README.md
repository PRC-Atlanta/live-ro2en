# Bible texts for the verse matcher

`src/live/verses.py` recognises a verse the speaker is reading or quoting and captions the
published text instead of a fragment-by-fragment translation. These files are its data; they
are generated by `scripts/fetch_bible.py`, committed, and bundled with the app
(`packaging/app_files.py`), because the app runs offline.

| File | Size | Content |
|---|---|---|
| `verses.tsv.gz` | 3.8 MB | `book chapter verse ro web kjv`, one row per verse (31,105 rows; book = 1-66 in the order of `data/bible_books.csv`) |
| `lexicon.tsv.gz` | 0.13 MB | `ro en dice`: Romanian/English word keys found in the same verses (Dice ≥ 0.15, best 6 partners per word), used to place a part of a verse in the other text |

Regenerate (downloads into the git-ignored `data/ref/bible/`, checks the sha256 pins, writes
the same bytes for the same input):

```bash
.venv/bin/python scripts/fetch_bible.py
```

### How the Romanian column is made

eBible.org's public-domain Cornilescu is in Cyrillic, which does not transliterate
unambiguously (я = *ea* or *ia*, ы = *â* or *î*). So each verse is taken from `ro_vdc.json`
(modern spelling, the church's text) when it is the same text as the ron1924 verse — a
phonetic comparison (vowels dropped, â/î, ea/ia, ch/c merged; similarity ≥ 0.9) — after
dropping its inlined explanatory notes ("Ben-Oni (Fiul durerii mele)" → "Ben-Oni") and the
spaces before punctuation:

- 30,996 verses checked equal to ron1924;
- 49 verses that differ are transliterated from ron1924 (`textnorm.cyr_to_latin`);
- 57 verses (John 11, missing from ron1924) come from `ro_vdc.json` unchecked.

### Changes to the English

- The WEB's VPL text starts verse 1 of 117 psalms with the title ("A Psalm by David. The
  LORD is my shepherd"); Cornilescu does not number titles, so they are cut, using the
  `\d` titles of the USFM edition.
- KJV: italics brackets and the acrostic letters of Psalm 119 are removed (matching only).
- At caption time `LORD`/`GOD` are written `Lord`/`God` like the app's other captions.

### Caveat on Cornilescu

eBible.org states that the 1924 Cornilescu is public domain; Bible Gateway lists it as
"© 1924 British and Foreign Bible Society", and Cornilescu died in 1975. The revised
editions (Cornilescu 2014 "VDCC", Romanian Bible Society) are copyrighted and are not used.
The church reads the 1924 text; if the app is distributed beyond the church, confirm the
status with the Bible Society or keep the `ro` column out of the published package.
